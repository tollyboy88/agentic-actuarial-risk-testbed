# Journal-selection research report

## Decision

The primary target is the **Scandinavian Actuarial Journal** (Taylor & Francis), using its standard subscription publication route. It is the closest scope match and its author instructions explicitly state that there are no submission, publication, or page charges on that route. Optional Open Select is not required and may carry an article processing charge.

## Why not make Oxford University Press the first choice?

Oxford University Press journals are not uniformly pay-to-publish: hybrid journals permit normal subscription publication when authors do not select optional open access. The Computer Journal is a plausible methods outlet, but it has a higher computer-science novelty threshold and its journal page is less explicit about zero standard-route fees. Law, Probability and Risk explicitly permits a no-charge standard route but is a poor scope fit without a substantial legal-risk contribution. Journal of Cybersecurity is fully open access and may charge an APC. These are avoidable uncertainties for this manuscript.

## Springer Nature alternative

The European Actuarial Journal is an excellent second choice. Its official fee page states that authors choosing the subscription route incur no APC; optional open access currently carries a charge. Its scope covers actuarial science, insurance risk, solvency, risk management, applications, and case studies. It is a credible transfer target if the Scandinavian Actuarial Journal declines the paper.

## Submission requirements implemented

- Main manuscript with an abstract below 200 words, six keywords, APA-style references, declarations, data-availability statement, and generative-AI disclosure.
- Separate title page / author-details file.
- An identified manuscript and a portable anonymized version, although the journal currently uses single-anonymous review.
- Editable Word tables on separate pages.
- Separate high-resolution figures and a figure-caption list.
- Cover letter and supplementary methods/results.
- Public reproducibility repository; restricted third-party raw data are not redistributed.

## Evidential boundary

“Free” means free to the author on the standard subscription route. The published article may be accessible only to subscribers unless an open-access agreement, waiver, repository route, or optional APC is used. Charges can change, so the author should re-open the official instructions immediately before submission and decline optional paid services unless desired.
